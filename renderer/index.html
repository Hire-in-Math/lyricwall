Welcome to LyricWall

Connect your Spotify in about 2 minutes. You only do this once.

Spotify requires Spotify Premium for apps like this, and each person makes their own free Spotify developer app.
  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Click “Create app.” Name and description can be anything (e.g. “LyricWall”).
  3. Paste this as the Redirect URI, then click Add: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
