Set up LyricWall

Install the app, choose where your music plays, and press play. If your music plays on this computer, you don't need Spotify Premium or any setup.

Install the app

  1. Download LyricWall for Mac

    One download works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 12 or newer.

    Download for Mac

  2. Drag it into Applications

    Open the downloaded LyricWall-Mac.dmg file and drag the LyricWall icon onto the Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    Open LyricWall from Applications. Your Mac will say it couldn't verify the app, because LyricWall isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate. Click Done, then:

    • Open System Settings and go to Privacy & Security.
    • Scroll down to the message about LyricWall and click Open Anyway.
    • Enter your password, then click Open.

    You only have to do this once.

    If your Mac says LyricWall “is damaged and can't be opened,” open Terminal, paste this, press Return, and open the app again:

    xattr -cr /Applications/LyricWall.app

  4. Allow system audio (for the beat)

    When your Mac asks to let LyricWall record system audio, click Allow. This lets the lights and artwork pulse to your music. Nothing is recorded or saved. If you skip it, LyricWall uses a steady pulse instead.

  1. Download LyricWall for Windows

    Works on Windows 10 and 11.

    Download for Windows

  2. Run the installer

    Open LyricWall-Windows-Setup.exe from your Downloads. Windows may show a blue box that says “Windows protected your PC,” because LyricWall is from a new developer. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Open LyricWall

    It installs in a few seconds and opens by itself. Next time, open it from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

Pick where your music plays

  1. Choose “On this computer”

    LyricWall follows Apple Music or the Spotify app, free or Premium. There's nothing else to set up.

  2. On a Mac, click OK

    The first time you play a song, macOS asks if LyricWall can control Music or Spotify. Click OK so it can see what's playing. If you clicked Don't Allow, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Automation, and turn on Music and Spotify under LyricWall.

That's it. Play a song and the lyrics show up. You can switch sources anytime in Settings, under App.

Optional: follow Spotify on any device

Choose “Spotify on any device” if your music plays on your phone, a speaker or another computer. This needs Spotify Premium, and Spotify only lets apps like LyricWall connect this way through a free developer app that you create yourself. It's quick, and you never have to touch it again.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard

    Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with the Spotify account you listen on. If it's your first time, accept the developer terms. Spotify may ask you to verify your email first.

  2. Create an app

    Click Create app and fill it in:

    • App name and App description: anything, like “LyricWall.”
    • Redirect URI: paste the address below exactly, then click Add.
    http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
    • Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?”, tick Web API.
    • Agree to the terms and click Save.

  3. Copy your Client ID

    On your new app's page, open Settings and copy the Client ID. It's a 32-character mix of letters and numbers. You don't need the Client Secret.

  4. Paste it into LyricWall

    Paste the Client ID into LyricWall's welcome screen and click Connect Spotify. A browser tab opens. Log in if asked, click Agree, then go back to LyricWall.

  5. Play a song

    Play anything on Spotify, on your computer, phone or speaker. LyricWall picks it up within a second or two.

Make it yours

Drag the window to your second monitor and press F for fullscreen. Press S to open Settings, where you can change the lyric style, light effect and colors, background, beat sensitivity and more.

Keyboard shortcuts
M switches between Lyrics, Cover and Vinyl. L changes the lyric style, E the light effect and C the light colors. Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys skip, T keeps the window on top, and [ ] nudge the lyric timing.

If something's not working

“INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI”
The Redirect URI in your Spotify app doesn't match. Open your app's Settings on the Spotify dashboard, make sure it's exactly http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback, and click Save.
“User not registered in the Developer Dashboard”
You're using someone else's Client ID. Create your own app with the steps above. Each Spotify developer app only works for its owner and up to five people they add.
It says “Open Apple Music or Spotify” while music is playing
LyricWall looks for the Music app or the Spotify app on this computer. If your music is playing in a web browser or on your phone, open Settings, go to App, and switch Music source to Spotify account (Premium).
It asks for permission to see what's playing (Mac)
Click Open System Settings on the LyricWall screen, or go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, Automation, and turn on Music and Spotify under LyricWall.
It says “Play something on Spotify” while music is playing
Make sure you're logged in to the same Spotify account you connected, and that Private Session is off. Then give it a few seconds.
Play, pause and skip don't work
With “On this computer”, make sure the Music or Spotify app is open. On Windows, change the volume in your music app. With a Spotify account, these need Premium and an active device, so start playback in Spotify once, then try again.
The lights don't react to the beat
On a Mac, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Screen & System Audio Recording, and turn on LyricWall. Press B in the app to try again.
The lyrics are early or late
Press [ or ] to shift them by a quarter second, or use the timing slider in Settings.
Start over
In Settings, use Log out to reconnect Spotify, or Reset settings to go back to the defaults.
