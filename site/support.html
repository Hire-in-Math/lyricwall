How can I help?

Hey! Is something broken, or do you have an idea for LyricWall?

Something's broken Report a bug. I read every one. I have an idea Suggest a lyric style, light effect or anything else. See what others posted Upvote ideas with a 👍 and check if your problem is already known.

Quick fixes

LyricWall says to connect Spotify again.

Spotify signed you out, which can happen if you changed your password or removed the app's access. Click Connect Spotify and sign in. Your settings are kept.

Nothing shows up when I play a song.

If your music source is “This computer”, make sure the song is playing in the Music app or the Spotify app, not a web browser. If it's “Spotify account”, check that Spotify is playing on some device and that you have Premium. Podcasts and ads don't have lyrics, so LyricWall shows a clock while they play.

My Mac asks to let LyricWall control Music or Spotify.

Click OK. That's how LyricWall sees what's playing. If you clicked Don't Allow, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Automation, and turn on Music and Spotify under LyricWall.

Some songs don't have lyrics.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free community database, and some songs aren't in it yet. LyricWall shows the album cover for those. You can change that in Settings, under Songs without lyrics.

The lyrics are early or late.

Press [ or ] while the song plays to nudge it by a quarter second. LyricWall remembers the fix for that song.

The lights don't move with the beat.

On a Mac, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Screen & System Audio Recording, and turn on LyricWall. Then press B in the app. On Windows, press B and make sure the Beat button is on.

Play, skip or volume don't work.

With “This computer”, keep the Music or Spotify app open. On Windows, change the volume in your music app. With “Spotify account”, Spotify only allows controls with Premium and an active device, and some speakers and TVs don't allow volume changes from other apps.

My Mac says the app "can't be opened."

Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. Step-by-step help.

Press ? in the app to see every keyboard shortcut.
